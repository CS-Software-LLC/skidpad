# Data provenance

Every data file used for validation or shipped as a preset must be listed here
with its source and licence. Files without an entry are not merged.

| File                                                         | What it is                                                                                                                                                                                              | Source                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Licence                                                                                               | Notes                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `crates/skidpad-core/tests/fixtures/synthetic_passenger.tir` | Magic Formula parameter set for a generic passenger tire                                                                                                                                                | Written for this project by hand; coefficients chosen to give a ~1.0 peak friction coefficient at ~7° slip angle and ~12% slip ratio at the nominal load                                                                                                                                                    | MIT OR Apache-2.0                                                                                     | Synthetic. Not measured data. Do not use for engineering decisions.                                                                  |
| `packages/presets/src/vehicles/*.json`                       | Reference vehicle definitions                                                                                                                                                                           | Mass, dimensions, and power figures from manufacturer press material and public spec sheets; tire feel parameters tuned to match published skidpad and braking figures                                                                                                                                      | MIT OR Apache-2.0                                                                                     | Each file carries a `dataSheet` field with its sources.                                                                              |
| `packages/presets/src/index.ts` (`surfaces`)                 | Reference surface table                                                                                                                                                                                 | Friction coefficients by surface from Wong, _Theory of Ground Vehicles_, ch. 1 and Gillespie, _Fundamentals of Vehicle Dynamics_, ch. 10; rolling resistance by surface from Wong; ploughing drag from the order of magnitude of motion resistance in Wong ch. 2                                            | MIT OR Apache-2.0                                                                                     | Each entry carries a `source` field. Class-representative, not measured.                                                             |
| `tests/determinism/data/lap-*.json`                          | Recorded input traces of the sandbox track                                                                                                                                                              | Produced by `tests/determinism/src/record-lap.ts` (a scripted driver) on the core at the commit that last changed the physics; re-recorded when a milestone moves the physics                                                                                                                               | MIT OR Apache-2.0                                                                                     | Inputs only; the replay is what the determinism check hashes.                                                                        |
| `tools/chrono-compare/reference/sedan/*`                     | Reference runs of Project Chrono's multibody Sedan, and its tire property file `Sedan_Pac02Tire.tir`                                                                                                    | Generated with PyChrono 9.0.1 (BSD-3-Clause) by `tools/chrono-compare/chrono/sedan.py` and `chrono/kc.py sedan`; the `.tir` is copied unchanged from Chrono 9.0.1's `data/vehicle/sedan/tire/` (BSD-3-Clause)                                                                                               | MIT OR Apache-2.0; the `.tir` BSD-3-Clause                                                            | Simulator output and simulator input data, not measured data. See `docs/validation/chrono-sedan.md`.                                 |
| `tools/chrono-compare/reference/bmw_e90/*`                   | Reference runs of Project Chrono's multibody BMW_E90 for the behavioural comparison                                                                                                                     | Generated with PyChrono 9.0.1 (BSD-3-Clause) by `tools/chrono-compare/chrono/bmw_e90.py`, adapted from the sibling project csummers88/vehicle-physics-new (MIT, same author)                                                                                                                                | MIT OR Apache-2.0                                                                                     | Simulator output, not measured data. See `docs/validation/chrono-bmw-e90.md`.                                                        |
| `tools/chrono-compare/src/chrono-e90.ts`                     | Constants of Chrono's BMW_E90: mass, inertia, hardpoints, design camber, rates, tire coefficients, engine map, gearing                                                                                  | Project Chrono 9.0.1 source, `src/chrono_models/vehicle/bmw/` (BSD-3-Clause); numbers only, each with its source file                                                                                                                                                                                       | MIT OR Apache-2.0                                                                                     | Used only to build the comparison vehicle, not a preset.                                                                             |
| `tools/chrono-compare/src/chrono-sedan.ts`                   | Constants of Chrono's Sedan: spring and damper rates and stops, inertias, brake torque, engine map, gearing                                                                                             | Project Chrono 9.0.1 source, `src/chrono_models/vehicle/sedan/` (BSD-3-Clause); numbers only, each with its source file                                                                                                                                                                                     | MIT OR Apache-2.0                                                                                     | Used only to build the comparison vehicle, not a preset.                                                                             |
| `tools/nhtsa-compare/reference/jeep_cherokee/*.csv`          | Measured responses of NHTSA VRTC's instrumented 1997 Jeep Cherokee Sport (hand wheel, lateral acceleration, yaw rate, roll, braking and acceleration runs), and the paper's NADSdyna simulation of them | Digitised by `tools/nhtsa-compare/digitise/digitise.py` from the vector plots of Salaani and Heydinger, "Model Validation of the 1997 Jeep Cherokee for the National Advanced Driving Simulator", SAE 2000-01-0700 (the copy NHTSA hosts as `jeep_valid.pdf`); the measurements are US government test data | Measured values (facts), MIT OR Apache-2.0 as digitised; the paper is © SAE and not in the repository | Measured data, read off published plots: within 0.5 % of a tick spacing on every axis. See `docs/validation/nhtsa-jeep-cherokee.md`. |
| `tools/nhtsa-compare/src/jeep.ts`                            | Specifications of the 1997 Jeep Cherokee Sport: wheelbase, track, steering ratio, curb mass, engine rating, gearing, tire size                                                                          | cars.com, auto123.com and automobile-catalog.com specification pages, Wikipedia (Jeep Cherokee (XJ), Aisin AW4), owners' forum figures for the springs, SAE 2000-01-0700 for the test vehicle; numbers only, each with its source in the file                                                               | MIT OR Apache-2.0                                                                                     | Every value without a published source is marked ESTIMATE. Used only to build the comparison vehicle, not a preset.                  |

Behavioural comparisons against other simulators (driving the same scenario and
comparing telemetry) are fine and should be recorded in `docs/validation/` with
the scenario, the other simulator's version, and the date.
