# Governance

Skidpad is maintainer-led until 1.0.

- **Maintainers** merge pull requests, cut releases, and own the roadmap in the
  README. Decisions that are not obvious are recorded as Architecture Decision
  Records in `docs/adr/` and are open for comment before they are accepted.
- **Contributors** are anyone who has had a pull request merged. Contributors
  with a track record of good reviews may be invited to become maintainers.
- **Disagreements** are resolved by discussion on the issue or ADR. If no
  consensus emerges, the maintainers decide and record the reasoning in the ADR.

After 1.0 this document will be revisited with the goal of moving to a small
steering group elected from active contributors.
